The Billion Tree Tsunami Project was launched in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan to restore depleted forests and mitigate climate change.

Project facts verified by independent auditors:
- Location: Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan
- Total Trees Planted: 1000000000 (1 Billion)
- Species: Native regional trees, no invasive species allowed.
- Estimated Carbon Sequestration: Approx 200,000,000 tons over the lifetime of the forest.
